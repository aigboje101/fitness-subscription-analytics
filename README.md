T10 Sprint 7 Project Analysis Summaries

Customer Cohort Analysis:

Using our table, we can see that the biggest drop in numbers happens within the first few months. The initial and first month gaps have similar drop rates amounting 1396 customers (15%) then 1349 customers (17%), but the second month to third month has the steepest drop at 2648 customers or 40%. With this data we now know that Customers will typically cancel after the second month.

Revenue Forecast Analysis:

Our chart shows an expected Revenue rise to almost 0.35M by the end of 2026. We can see evidence of seasonal trends in the dips of the graph. Revenue should be at its lowest during January.

CAC vs. LTV:

2024 Cohorts were able to recover from their CAC during July 2024, although there was a recent drop to under the CAC during December 2025. The 2025 Cohorts only recovered during august 2025.
