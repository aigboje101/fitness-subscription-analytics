T10 Sprint 7 Project Analysis Summaries

Customer Cohort Analysis:

Using our table, we can see that the biggest drop in numbers happens within the first few months. The initial and first month gaps have similar drop rates amounting in a 15% drop (84.99% retention), then a 17% drop after month one (71.56% retention), but the second month to third month has the steepest drop at 29.55% resulting in a 42.01% Retention Rate. With this data we now know that Customers will typically cancel after the second month.

Revenue Forecast Analysis:

Our chart shows an expected Revenue rise to almost 0.35M by the end of 2026. We can see evidence of seasonal trends in the dips of the graph. Revenue should be at its lowest during January.

CAC vs. LTV:
